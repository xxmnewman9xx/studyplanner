## Launch Blog Draft

Title: Back to School with AI: Build Your Semester Plan Before Classes Begin

Study Planner AI's Back-to-School update is designed for the moment before classes begin, when a student has a blank planner, a stack of syllabi, and a lot of uncertainty. The new setup flow turns that first-week pressure into a guided sequence: add your name, import or enter schoolwork, review the plan, then start from a dashboard that already knows what matters next.

The goal is not to hide decisions behind automation. Students stay in control by reviewing detected classes, assignments, exams, and uncertain dates before anything reaches the live dashboard, reminders, widgets, or calendar-ready plan.

The release also introduces a stronger widget story. The Semester Calendar widget preview shows the week at a glance, including busy days, exam markers, and pressure signals, so students can see what is coming before they open the app.

This update positions Study Planner AI as an intelligent semester planning assistant: a way to organize the semester early, reduce setup stress, and avoid last-minute cramming before it starts.
