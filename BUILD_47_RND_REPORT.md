# Build 47 R&D Report

## Sources Reviewed
- Apple HIG onboarding: fast, interactive onboarding should help people start using the app quickly.
- Apple HIG in-app purchase: subscription benefits should be clear during onboarding and StoreKit should handle purchase/restore patterns.
- Apple App Review Guidelines 3.1.2: subscriptions need clear terms, ongoing value, no scam/bait-and-switch behavior, and cross-device availability.
- Apple WidgetKit: widgets should be focused, glanceable, timely, and personally relevant.
- Competitive patterns from Apple Fitness, Apple Sports, Cal AI, Structured, Todoist, Duolingo, Finch, Notion Calendar, K-12 planners, college planners, exam prep, and AI study tools.

## Principles Extracted
- Onboarding conversion: lead with one outcome, not setup. Ask only what changes the first experience.
- Hard paywall timing: show value promise, then lock. Do not let users reach premium workflows before entitlement.
- Universal student positioning: avoid college-only language; “school semester,” “exams,” “online classes,” and “notes” work across K-12, college, grad, and professional students.
- Subscription value framing: sell clarity, control, reminders, widgets, and ongoing semester coaching.
- Simplicity: remove theme choice and customization. A finished product should feel opinionated.
- Widget clarity: one state, one driver, one next move. No mini dashboards.
- App Review risk: keep restore visible, terms/privacy visible, price delegated to StoreKit when unavailable, no misleading AI claims.

## Build 47 Direction
One product. One visual system. One hard paywall. One promise: Know your semester.
