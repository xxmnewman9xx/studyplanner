## What To Borrow
- Apple Fitness: one dominant state, progress that maps honestly to effort, restrained celebration.
- Apple Sports: live, personalized status surfaces that answer the primary question immediately.
- Whoop: explainable scores with one driver, not analytics overload.
- Nike Run Club: coaching without chat. The next action should feel guided, not managed.
- Duolingo/Finch: a daily reason to return, but without streak anxiety or childish visuals.
- Todoist: visible progress and history, but not a productivity scoreboard.

## What To Avoid
- Rainbow category systems that make every class compete with health status.
- Generic todo copy like “upcoming deadlines” or “study more.”
- Paywalls before the user sees the semester value.
- Dashboard density that forces interpretation.
- Streak pressure that makes stressed students feel worse.

## StudyPlanner Differentiation
StudyPlanner should own the semester-level emotional promise:

Syllabus in. Semester out. Coach active.

The daily loop is not “complete tasks.” It is:
- Know your semester state.
- Understand the driver.
- Take the next move.
- See the semester improve.

## Build 44 Doctrine
- Health color is status only.
- Category identity comes from iconography and layout.
- The state label should be stronger than the number.
- Global syllabus and notes parsing must degrade to review, never hallucinate certainty.
- Widgets should sell the app while closed: state, score, driver, next move.
