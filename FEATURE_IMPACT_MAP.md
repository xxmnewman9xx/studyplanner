# Feature Impact Map

## Scan

Creates the semester.

Role in Pulse:

- captures syllabus, PDF, photo, or typed work
- creates candidate classes, assignments, exams, and dates
- starts the Pulse only after the user has work to trust

Success:

- student understands "scan first"
- no unreviewed work pollutes Home, widgets, or Watch

## Review Inbox

Verifies the semester.

Role in Pulse:

- protects trust before imported items affect the score
- raises confidence when dates, duplicates, and uncertain rows are accepted
- explains why widgets may wait

Success:

- review count is treated as Pulse confidence, not admin clutter

## Home

Protects the Pulse.

Role in Pulse:

- starts with Semester Pulse
- shows one next best action
- explains the top reason
- supports with today cards only after the Pulse decision
- shows a win or momentum loop

Success:

- student knows what to do before scrolling

## Forecast

Predicts the Pulse.

Role in Pulse:

- turns work clusters into Calm, Building, Heavy, Peak, or Recovery
- shows peak day/week, top risk, exam pressure, free time, and action
- builds trust with trend and local forecast memory

Success:

- student acts before the week becomes urgent

## Focus

Improves the Pulse.

Role in Pulse:

- converts anxiety into a timer action
- records real effort
- tunes future block sizes
- creates wins after completion

Success:

- completing a focus block visibly improves momentum

## Classes

Explains the Pulse.

Role in Pulse:

- shows which class is driving risk
- connects open work, exams, notes, and focus history
- makes class colors/icons useful across app, widgets, and Watch

Success:

- student knows which course is pulling the semester off track

## Notes

Supports class progress and study.

Role in Pulse:

- captures class context that can support next actions
- links notes to tasks, focus, and courses
- makes studying produce reusable memory

Success:

- notes help the next task, not just archive text

## Widgets

Broadcast the Pulse.

Role in Pulse:

- show Semester Pulse, next assignment, exam countdown, future risk, focus window, or class progress
- use reviewed planner data only
- make Home Screen setup practical

Success:

- widget choice is obvious from what the student needs to glance

## Apple Watch

Glances the Pulse.

Role in Pulse:

- shows Pulse, next due, exam countdown, focus window, and today progress
- avoids dense lists
- answers "what matters next?" on the wrist

Success:

- user can decide in one wrist glance

## Studio

Personalizes colors, classes, widgets, and accents.

Role in Pulse:

- changes class colors/icons that carry through cards, widgets, and Watch
- recommends practical widgets based on current Pulse need
- saves widget presets with data source and surface

Success:

- Studio answers "What should I put on my Home Screen?"

## Paywall

Sells advanced Pulse, forecast, widgets, Watch, and imports.

Role in Pulse:

- frames paid value around syllabus import, review trust, forecast, widgets, Watch, and local adaptation
- avoids selling generic planner features

Success:

- subscription value is the semester control system, not a cosmetic upgrade
