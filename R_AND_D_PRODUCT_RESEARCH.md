# R&D Product Research - Semester Pulse

## Objective

StudyPlanner: Syllabus AI should not copy planner competitors. The product move is to turn syllabus import plus student work history into one trusted semester state: **Semester Pulse**.

The student should understand in under 3 seconds:

- where they stand
- what matters next
- whether trouble is building
- what action improves the semester

## Student And Planner Competitors

**MyStudyLife** emphasizes timetable setup, homework, exams, revision planning, Pomodoro, widgets, reminders, dark mode, and cross-device sync. Its strength is full student coverage; its weakness is that it can still read as a pile of planner modules. Source: [MyStudyLife App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/my-study-life-school-planner/id910639339), [MyStudyLife](https://mystudylife.com/).

**Structured** turns a day into one visual timeline. The retention lesson is clarity: tasks and calendar commitments collapse into one visible day instead of separate databases. Source: [Structured](https://structured.app/).

**Todoist** creates daily return through Today, Upcoming, cross-surface capture, and Karma/progress. The caution is that points can become abstract if they are not tied to real outcome. Source: [Todoist features](https://www.todoist.com/features), [Todoist productivity view](https://get.todoist.help/hc/en-us/articles/360000410829-How-to-use-the-Productivity-view).

**Google Calendar** wins because it is already the shared source of time truth. Its old Goals model and newer task-blocking direction point to a key lesson: tasks become real when they claim calendar time. Source: [Google Calendar Goals](https://blog.google/products/calendar/find-time-goals-google-calendar/).

**Notion Calendar** connects databases, notes, meetings, and availability. Lesson: calendar is strongest when it links to the work object. Avoid making users build their own system before getting value. Source: [Notion Calendar with Notion](https://www.notion.com/help/use-notion-calendar-with-notion), [Notion Calendar guide](https://www.notion.com/en-gb/help/guides/getting-started-with-notion-calendar).

**Motion** and **Reclaim** sell adaptive scheduling and protected focus time. Lesson: users like the promise that the system defends time, but trust breaks when automation feels opaque or overwrites intent. Source: [Motion AI Calendar](https://www.usemotion.com/ai-calendar), [Reclaim features](https://help.reclaim.ai/en/articles/6210740-features-in-reclaim).

**Power Planner** focuses on assignments, class schedules, grades, GPA, calendar sync, widgets, and reminders. Lesson: grade/progress context matters for students, but a planner can feel utilitarian unless it creates a state users care about. Source: [Power Planner](https://powerplanner.net/).

**Schooly and similar student planners** foreground class schedules, assignments, exams, attendance, notes, colors, themes, and widgets. Lesson: students expect color and personalization, but themes alone are not a product moat. Source: [Schooly App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/schooly-school-planner-todo/id1500232555).

## Best-In-Class Product References

**Apple Sports** is fast and simple: favorite teams, live state, scores, stats, standings, widgets, and Live Activities. Lesson: Home should feel like a scoreboard for the semester, not a feed. Source: [Apple Sports support](https://support.apple.com/en-me/guide/apple-sports-app/apdec6a74b4c/web), [Apple Sports App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/apple-sports/id6446788829).

**Apple Fitness and Activity Rings** turn progress into a daily state, then deepen it with trends, awards, sharing, and weekly summaries. Lesson: one iconic object can anchor retention when it is understandable, earned, and visible everywhere. Source: [Apple Watch Activity](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT205406), [Apple Watch activity newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/04/get-active-with-apple-watch/).

**Apple Wallet** is valuable because it surfaces the right pass at the moment needed on iPhone and Watch. Lesson: widgets and Watch should not be secondary dashboards; they should be passes for the next school moment. Source: [Apple Wallet](https://www.apple.com/wallet/), [Wallet developer overview](https://developer.apple.com/wallet/).

**Apple Photos Memories** turns personal history into emotionally legible highlights. Lesson: StudyPlanner wins attachment when it reflects a student back to themselves: wins, recovery, improved habits, and semester story. Source: [Apple Photos Memories](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/guide/photos/pht96259d626/mac).

**Cal AI** is a simplicity reference: scan, get a result, log faster than typing. Lesson: syllabus import should feel like "scan, review, trust," not a chatbot. Source: [Cal AI](https://calai.me/), [Cal AI App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cal-ai-food-calorie-tracker/id6480417616).

**Duolingo** uses streaks, leagues, badges, and progress. Lesson: a daily loop works when the action is tiny and the progress is protected. Avoid streak guilt for school; use wins and recovery instead. Source: [Duolingo streak blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/improving-the-streak/), [Duolingo achievement badges](https://blog.duolingo.com/achievement-badges/).

**Spotify Wrapped** shows that users share personalized data when it feels true, specific, and expressive. Lesson: StudyPlanner should build toward semester recaps and weekly wins, but avoid creepy or inaccurate claims. Source: [Spotify Wrapped support](https://support.spotify.com/de-en/article/spotify-wrapped/), [Spotify Wrapped newsroom](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2024-12-04/the-art-and-science-behind-spotify-wrapped/).

## Answers To Research Questions

### What Creates Daily Retention?

- A single state worth checking: Pulse score, status, trend.
- One next action with a reason.
- External surfaces that answer without opening the app.
- Small wins after completion, review, focus, and recovery.
- Forecast warnings early enough to act.

### What Creates Emotional Attachment?

- The app remembers real behavior locally: focus duration, completed work, notes, saved widgets.
- The user sees a personal semester story: "You started Chemistry earlier than usual."
- Progress is earned, not decorative.
- Recovery is celebrated, not punished.

### What Gives Users A Score Or State?

- Apple Fitness: ring closure and trends.
- Apple Sports: score/state of the game.
- Todoist: Karma and productivity goals.
- Duolingo: streak and course progress.
- StudyPlanner's version: Semester Pulse, a health score from workload, deadlines, focus, review trust, and class risk.

### What Is Glanceable In Under 3 Seconds?

- Score: 82.
- Status: On Track.
- Trend: +6 this week.
- Reason: Thursday is overloaded.
- Action: Start Chemistry tonight.

### What Becomes Better After 30 Days?

- Recommended focus duration.
- Class risk ranking.
- Widget recommendation.
- Watch signal timing.
- Wins and recovery copy.
- Forecast trend confidence.

### What Would A User Screenshot Or Show A Friend?

- A high Pulse score after a rough week.
- A recovery state after clearing overdue work.
- A weekly win strip: "You finished 4 assignments this week."
- A forecast card showing a peak week before it happens.
- A personalized widget recommendation with clear reason.

### What Should StudyPlanner Avoid Copying?

- Generic task-list cards.
- Theme galleries without functional consequence.
- Fake AI chat.
- Opaque auto-scheduling.
- Streak pressure that punishes students during illness, work, or family load.
- Social competition. Students need control and relief first.

## Product Principles Extracted

1. One signature object beats many modules.
2. Every surface should answer one question: "What does this do to my Pulse?"
3. Import trust is a product feature, not a setup chore.
4. Forecast should feel like weather: simple state, risk, peak, action.
5. Widgets and Watch should be passes for the next school moment.
6. Personalization must change data, color, or decisioning. Otherwise delete it.
7. Motivation should reward control, early starts, recovery, and completed work.
