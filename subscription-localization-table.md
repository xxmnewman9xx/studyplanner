## Compliance Checks

- subscription value is described as higher limits and extra tools, not unlimited access.
- Renewal language is present in every locale.
- Prices are delegated to the App Store display price.
- No locale claims OCR, camera parsing, AI automation, LMS sync, or guaranteed syllabus extraction.
